Getting Here

However you're travelling.

Nearest car park

Silbury Boulevard multi-storey, a two-minute walk. Pay on exit.

Long appointments

Braids can run five hours or more — buy a full-day ticket rather than topping up.

Sat nav

Use the postcode MK9 3AG, then follow signs for Silbury Arcade.

Milton Keynes Central

About a ten-minute walk, straight up Midsummer Boulevard.

Bus station

MK Central bus station is five minutes away on foot.

Late finishes

If your appointment runs past the last bus, tell us — we will get you a taxi number.

Step-free access

Level entry from the arcade, no steps anywhere inside the salon.

Adjustable chairs

Two stations take a wheelchair transfer, and we have a reclining basin.

Quieter times

Ask for a Tuesday or Wednesday morning if you would prefer a calmer salon.

Tell us what you need when you book and we will set the space up before you arrive. Nothing is too much trouble to ask.

Children welcome

Bring them along. There is a kids' corner and we build breaks into their appointments.

Paying

Card, cash or bank transfer. The deposit can be paid by transfer when you book.

Long sittings

Tea, water and a phone charger are free. Bring headphones and something to watch.

Ready when you are.

Request an appointment and we will come back with times that fit the style you want.
