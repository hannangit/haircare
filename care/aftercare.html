Looking after it at home.

A style is only as good as the six weeks that follow it. Everything below applies whether we did your hair or someone else did.

Every night, whatever your style

This is the one rule that applies to everyone, and the one most people skip.

  • Satin or silk bonnet, scarf or pillowcase. Cotton pulls moisture straight out of your hair while you sleep.
  • Tie long braids or locs loosely — a tight nightly ponytail causes the same tension damage as a tight install.
  • Do not sleep on wet hair, especially in braids or locs. It is how mildew and smell start.

Washing a protective style

Yes, you should still wash it. Six weeks of unwashed scalp is worse for your hair than any wash-day frizz.

  • Dilute your shampoo in a bottle with water and apply it to the scalp only, in the partings.
  • Massage with the pads of your fingers, never your nails.
  • Rinse in the direction the braids fall. Do not scrub the lengths.
  • Squeeze out water with a microfibre towel, then dry completely. A cool hairdryer is fine and often necessary.
  • Every two to three weeks is about right for most styles.
Not confident doing it yourself? A maintenance wash in the salon is £15 and takes twenty minutes. Book one.

Scalp care, and what is not normal

Some tightness on the first night is normal. Beyond that, it is not.

Normal:

  • Mild tightness for the first 24 hours after a fresh install.
  • A little flaking in the first week as the scalp settles.

Not normal — come back in:

  • Pain that has not eased after 48 hours.
  • Bumps, pus or broken skin, particularly along the hairline.
  • Hair coming out at the root in the partings.
  • Edges visibly thinner than before the style went in.
We will loosen, remove or reinstall the affected sections free of charge if the tension came from us. Get in touch.

Moisture — the short version

  • Water moisturises. Oil does not. Oil seals in moisture that is already there.
  • Mist with water or a water-based leave-in every two or three days, then seal with a light oil.
  • Heavy greases and thick butters on the scalp cause build-up, itching and smell. Use them sparingly, on the lengths.
  • Deep condition on every wash day when your hair is loose.
By style

Braids & twists

  • Take them out at 6–8 weeks. Longer than that and your own hair starts matting at the root, which is where breakage comes from.
  • Do not re-tighten the front braids yourself when they loosen — that is what causes traction alopecia at the hairline.
  • Trim frizz with small scissors rather than pulling it out.
  • Give your hair a week loose, with a deep conditioning treatment, before the next protective style.

Locs

  • Leave starter locs alone for the first four weeks. No twisting, no picking, no product experiments.
  • Retwist every four to six weeks — no more often. Over-retwisting thins the roots.
  • Use residue-free shampoo. Anything creamy will sit inside the loc and never come out.
  • Dry thoroughly, every single time.

Weaves & wigs

  • Wash the braids underneath every two weeks and dry them completely.
  • Take a sew-in out at eight weeks at the very latest.
  • Take your wig off at night and store it on a stand, not folded in a drawer.
  • Remove adhesive with a proper remover. Pulling a frontal off dry takes your hairline with it.

Relaxed & heat-styled hair

  • No washing for 72 hours after a relaxer.
  • Retouch roots only, and no sooner than ten weeks. Overlapping onto already-relaxed hair is the main cause of breakage.
  • Protein and moisture treatments in alternate weeks.
  • After a silk press, keep away from steam and rain, and wrap at night.
Salon terms

These apply to every appointment. They are here so nothing is a surprise on the day.

1 Consultations & quotes

  • Every service begins with a free consultation, in person or by video call.
  • Listed prices are starting prices. The final price depends on length, size and density, and is agreed with you before we begin — never after.
  • If we think a style will damage your hair, we will say so and offer an alternative. We reserve the right to decline work we believe is unsafe.

2 Deposits

  • A £20 deposit secures your appointment and comes off the final price.
  • The deposit is taken only after a time has been agreed with you.
  • Appointments over four hours may require a larger deposit at peak times; we will tell you before you pay.

3 Changes, cancellations & lateness

  • 48 hours' notice or more: your deposit moves to the new date.
  • Less than 48 hours, or a no-show: the deposit is retained.
  • More than 20 minutes late: we may need to shorten the style or rebook you, and the deposit stands.
  • If we cancel on you for any reason, your deposit is refunded in full.

4 Patch tests & health

  • Relaxers, texturisers, lash extensions, tints and bonded installs require a patch test at least 48 hours beforehand. This is free and takes two minutes.
  • We cannot carry out these services without one, even if you have had them elsewhere.
  • Please tell us if you are pregnant, taking medication that affects your hair or scalp, or have had a reaction to hair products before.
  • We are stylists, not clinicians. Anything that looks infected should be seen by a pharmacist or GP first.

5 Extension hair

  • Where a service says "bring your own", the service page tells you roughly how many packs you need. Bring extra rather than too little.
  • We can source hair for you at cost if you ask when booking, but we need three days' notice.
  • We cannot guarantee results with hair we have not supplied, particularly synthetic hair of unknown quality.

6 Children

  • Under-16s must be accompanied by an adult for the whole appointment.
  • Children's styles are done with lighter tension and shorter sittings, with breaks built in.
  • We will stop an appointment if a child is distressed, and we will not charge you for the time.

7 If you are not happy

  • Tell us within 7 days and we will put right anything that is our workmanship — free of charge.
  • That includes tension that is too tight, uneven partings, and installs that have not held as they should.
  • It does not cover a change of mind about a style you approved at the consultation, or damage caused by aftercare.
  • The full process is on our feedback and complaints page.
Reactions — what to do
  • Stop using any new product immediately.
  • Rinse the area with cool water. Do not scrub.
  • For itching or swelling around the eyes, face or throat, or any difficulty breathing, call 999.
  • For anything milder that is not settling, speak to a pharmacist, then let us know so we can note it on your record.
