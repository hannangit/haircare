A clear, four-step booking.

Found a style you like? Here is exactly what happens next — including what we need from you, and what we will not ask for until the day.

1

Choose & ask

Send a request for the service you want, or ask us to help you pick.

2

Free consultation

In person or on a video call, so we can see your hair before we quote.

3

Deposit & slot

A £20 deposit holds the chair and comes off the final price.

4

Prep & appointment

We send your prep list, then we see you on the day.

Turning up prepared is the single biggest thing that shortens your appointment and makes the style last.

  • 1
    Wash and blow-dry the day before — clean, stretched, product-free hair braids far faster than freshly oiled hair.
  • 2
    Detangle from the ends up. Come with your hair in loose sections if you can.
  • 3
    Bring your extension hair where the service needs it. Each service page tells you how many packs.
  • 4
    Take your last style out fully before you arrive. Take-downs are charged separately if we do them.
  • 5
    Eat before a long appointment. Braids can run five hours or more — bring a drink and headphones.

Not sure about any of this? Message us before your appointment and we'll talk it through.

Booking Deposit
£20

Holds your chair, and comes straight off the price of your service on the day.

Securing your appointment

  • The deposit is taken after we have agreed a time — never before, and never to browse.
  • The balance is paid on the day by card, cash or bank transfer.
  • Appointments over four hours may need a larger deposit at busy times; we will always say so up front.
Changes and cancellations:
  • Move or cancel with 48 hours' notice and your deposit carries over to the new date.
  • Under 48 hours, or a no-show, and the deposit is kept — it covers the chair we held empty.
  • Arriving more than 20 minutes late may mean we have to shorten or rebook the style.
  • If we ever have to cancel on you, your deposit is refunded in full.

Some services legally and sensibly need a patch test at least 48 hours before your appointment.

  • Relaxers and texturisers — plus a strand test on the day.
  • Lash extensions — for the adhesive.
  • Brow and lash tints — for the dye.
  • Bonded wig installs — for the adhesive.

Patch tests take two minutes and are free. Pop in any time we are open, or ask us to post one out. We cannot carry out these services without one, even if you have had them elsewhere.

Tell us in advance if you:
  • are pregnant, or have had a baby in the last six months
  • are on medication that affects your hair or scalp
  • have had a reaction to hair products before
  • have any scalp condition, tenderness or hair loss you are worried about
